**SQL (MySQL) • Power BI**

Analyzed 3 years of Oregon state transportation spending to identify trends, top vendors, and departmental patterns.

**What I built:**

### 3. **Brazil's Ethanol Industry: 50-Year Data-Driven Analysis**
**SQL • Power BI • Excel**

Comprehensive analysis of Brazil's biofuel industry (1975-2024) examining economic growth, job creation, and environmental impact—with implications for India's energy policy.

**What I built:**
- Aggregated 50 years of heterogeneous data sources (employment, production, emissions, vehicle sales)
- Cleaned and reconciled conflicting data from multiple government & industry databases
- Performed time-series analysis to identify inflection points: flex-fuel mandate (2003), financial crisis (2015), pandemic recovery (2021)
- Created multi-page Power BI dashboard with drill-through capabilities and KPI cards

**Key findings:**
- 751K jobs created across ethanol ecosystem (63% in agriculture)
- 828.7M tonnes CO2 avoided since 1975 vs. gasoline baseline
- Flex-fuel adoption accelerated from 2003 to 79.9% of new car sales by 2024
- Emissions reduced 39-46% compared to pure gasoline vehicles

**Why I built this:** Demonstrates ability to tackle complex, real-world analytical problems requiring data from multiple sources, synthesis of opposing viewpoints, and clear communication of findings to non-technical stakeholders.
